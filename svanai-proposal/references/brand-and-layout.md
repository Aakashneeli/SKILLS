# SvanAI brand and layout

## Identity

- Brand: `svanAI`
- Legal entity: `SvanAI Technologies LLP`
- Website: `svanai.com`
- Wordmark: render `svan` in the page foreground color and `AI` in green.

## Page system

- A4 portrait
- dark cover; white interior pages
- consistent header and footer rails
- numbered green section badges
- one primary narrative section per page when practical
- flexible page count; split dense sections and merge short sections after visual inspection

## Palette

- ink: `#121212`
- accent green: `#00E673`
- dark card: `#1B1B1B`
- dark green callout: `#102018`
- pale green: `#E5FCF0`
- soft row: `#F8F9FA`
- card gray: `#F2F3F5`
- rule gray: `#E4E6EA`
- body gray: `#333333`

## Typography

- Plus Jakarta Sans: display and section headings
- Inter: body and tables
- Roboto Mono: labels, metadata, prices, badges, and page furniture

Use the bundled font assets. Preserve hierarchy through size, whitespace, alignment, and restrained weight rather than decorative effects.

## Components

Use modular cards, alternating-row tables, comparison grids, timelines, milestone cards, and pale-green callouts. Keep tables readable, card padding consistent, and accent green purposeful. Avoid decorative charts unless they materially clarify a commercial relationship.

## Cover

Include proposal type, version/date, project title and outcome subtitle, prepared-for details, legal issuer, document status, currency/tax basis, proposal basis, and an indicative or final commercial note.
