# SvanAI content standard

## Voice

Write concise, commercially confident, outcome-first, evidence-bound English. Be consultative rather than sales-heavy. Use technical detail only when it helps the client or the selected branch. Prefer concrete business consequences over feature inventories.

## Narrative

Move in this order:

1. establish the client problem and desired outcome;
2. show that SvanAI understands the operating context;
3. explain the controlled solution or engagement;
4. freeze scope, acceptance, assumptions, and responsibilities;
5. show a credible delivery plan;
6. state the investment and payment logic;
7. reduce perceived delivery risk;
8. make the next decision simple.

## Evidence rules

- Bind every number, accuracy claim, saving, timeline assertion, client fact, and POC result to an intake source.
- State confirmed facts plainly.
- Label estimates as indicative.
- Put assumptions and `TBC` markers only in drafts.
- Distinguish an aspiration from a contractual acceptance measure.

## Why svanAI standard

Use these themes when relevant, tailoring the evidence without changing their meaning:

- Proven before you commit
- Domain understanding
- Collaborative and fast
- Materially lower risk
- Built to grow with you
- Outcome-first engineering

Working principles: clear communication and quick iteration; practical over complex; respect for deadlines and change control; client business outcome first.

## Scope standard

Pair every inclusion with enough detail to test whether it was delivered. Pair exclusions with their reason or the future phase in which they belong. State acceptance criteria, assumptions, dependencies, and client responsibilities close to scope.

## Commercial standard

Include detailed development costs. Show phase, description, hours, approved client billing rate, and amount when the user selects transparent phase/hour presentation. Keep internal costs and margins out of client output. Add packages only after explicit confirmation.

## Responsibilities standard

State both SvanAI and client responsibilities in operational terms: inputs, credentials, access, reviewer, feedback cadence, approvals, testing, handover, and support ownership. Align every responsibility to the roadmap and acceptance process.
