# SvanAI legal and commercial boilerplate

Use the following as the authoritative starting point. Preserve meaning and show any proposed change to the user before applying it.

## Standard clauses

### Support and warranty

A standard warranty period of 30 days after go-live covers defects in delivered functionality. Handle support through an agreed communication channel and track requests to closure. Manage enhancements or scope expansion through a formal change request.

### Payment terms

Invoices are payable within 14 days of issue and align to the approved milestone schedule.

### Confidentiality

Both parties keep non-public information shared during the engagement confidential. The obligation survives completion.

### Client data

Client data, drawings, and outputs remain the client's property. Do not use them to train or improve any model without prior written consent. Retain them only as required for delivery.

### Limitation of liability

Aggregate liability is capped at the total fees paid under the engagement. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

### Termination

Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. Fees for work performed to the termination date become due.

### Taxes

Fees are exclusive of GST and other applicable taxes, statutory levies, and third-party charges unless expressly included.

### Validity

The proposal is valid for 30 days from issue unless extended in writing.

### Governing law

The engagement is governed by the laws of India, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of India.

## IP choices

Ask the user to select one mode:

1. `client-owned`: assign project-specific deliverables after full payment; SvanAI retains pre-existing methodologies, architectures, tools, and reusable components.
2. `svanai-owned-license`: SvanAI retains ownership; define the client's license, term, territory, permitted use, and restrictions.
3. `custom`: use only wording approved for that engagement.
