# Quality gates

## Intake gate

- Every applicable field has a status and source.
- Research was performed only after consent.
- Contradictions and low-confidence interpretations were resolved or disclosed.
- The consolidated brief was approved.

## Client-ready gate

For `CLIENT-READY FINAL`, confirm client identity, scope, price, dates, currency/taxes, metrics, acceptance, IP mode, and legal terms. A draft may retain visibly marked assumptions or `TBC` fields.

## Commercial gate

- Workbook status is `PASS`.
- Phase totals equal selected client price.
- Taxes and grand total reconcile.
- Proposal price, package table, milestones, and commercial note agree.
- Scope, hours, timeline, responsibilities, and acceptance describe the same work.
- Internal costs, margins, contingency logic, and price comparisons do not appear in client artifacts.

## Content gate

- Every quantitative or factual claim is source-bound.
- Branch-specific content is present; irrelevant content is absent.
- Inclusions, exclusions, assumptions, responsibilities, and acceptance are testable.
- Technical detail earns its place for this client.
- Legal changes were approved and international terms are flagged for legal review.

## Visual gate

Render every page to PNG and inspect at readable scale. Pass only when:

- no content clips, overlaps, or crosses page boundaries;
- no broken glyphs, black squares, placeholder tokens, or encoding artifacts appear;
- tables, cards, and callouts align consistently;
- dense pages remain legible and sparse pages do not look accidental;
- cover, headers, footers, section badges, and page numbers are consistent;
- the PDF contains exactly the latest approved content.
